### D37.1 · 2026-10-30 · Standby in its own window (F37)
Status: built 2026-10-30; on the Portal (a real Home hold through a virtual key device,
`gotchas.md` *Testing a Home hold*): opens black, four A wake back to the app that was in front
(DroidDeck) or to Home as it was, extra presses land nowhere. Owner to try with a game.

**Why** (C2, H3): as a layer of Home, standby brought Home's task in front of the game, so waking
left the owner on Home, and its presses could reach Home underneath (P2: a fifth quick A
launched the focused tile; H8: Home kept drawing ripples and pulses under the black). A
separate activity in its own task returns to whatever is under it with nothing to remember.
**What**: `StandbyActivity` (own task, single instance, out of Recents, no history, opaque,
never over the lock screen: locked, a hold is a short press, H11). The wake guard (P2): after
the fourth press it stays as a key sink at least 0.4 s and until the waking key is let go, at
most 1 s. While it shows, `HomeHoldService` hands it short Home presses as wake presses and a
hold does nothing. One `Sounds` for the process, so standby's chime needs no second pool.
**Rejected**: relaunching the last launched app on wake (wrong for a game started from a
frontend or Recents); a translucent window (the app under it keeps drawing); a timer-only guard
(releases while the key is held) or a release-only guard (hangs on a lost UP).

### D38.1 · 2026-10-30 · One Home, cleaner starts (F38)
Status: built 2026-10-30; on the Portal: `am start -n` and the gear start reuse the one Home.

**Why** (H4): Android makes an activity Home only from the Home intent with no component, so
Portal's icon in another launcher, `am start -n` or the Default apps gear built a second
`MainActivity` in a plain task; its in-memory setup then wrote over Home's.
**What**: a start that isn't the Home intent, while Portal holds the role, hands over to Home
through that intent (`EXTRA_OPEN`) and finishes before anything is built. E7: Settings → System
→ Home app asks with Android's dialog (one A) when Portal isn't Home, then Default apps if it
still isn't; the value reads Another app when one is. H6: a cold start shows the backdrop's
color, then holds the first frame for the app list (at most 1 s), no gray, no empty row.
H7/F5: font size, bold text and locale no longer rebuild Home (process-death restore is D11.2).
**Rejected**: re-asking for the role on every open (nags people trying Portal from Launcher3).

### D39.1 · 2026-10-30 · Input that can't misfire (F39)
Status: built 2026-10-30; the lifecycle gate seen on the Portal (six quick A over standby).

**What and why**: H2, keys and touches do nothing while Home isn't resumed, so a second A in the
gap between a launch and the app's window can't open another app (the lifecycle is the only
gate, no timer); the launch sound plays only once the start succeeded, a failure bumps beside
"Couldn't open <app>". C1 (= E1, P1, H1, four readers found it): the Home key mid-rename left
the keyboard flag set and the controller dead; Home now finishes the rename as B does, and keys
reach a text field only while it is on screen. C10: an Android page that is missing, not
exported or refused shows a toast, never a crash. P3/P4: a held bumper skips one track or turns
one category, a held Left/Right on a settings value changes it once (Theme flashed light and
dark 20 times a second); lists, sliders and All apps' letter jumps keep repeating.
**Rejected**: a "launching" flag with a timeout (four ways to clear it, one to forget).

### D40.1 · 2026-10-30 · The left stick (F40)
Status: built 2026-10-30; the feel is the owner's to judge on the hardware.

**Why** (P7): Android's stick-to-D-pad keys fire a diagonal as both directions (a drop into the
dock while moving along the row) and repeat at one speed.
**What**: `Stick.kt` reads the left stick while the hat rests and Android holds no stick push of
its own; only the dominant axis moves, a push past half steps once, held it repeats faster the
further it is pushed; the steps go through every gate a key does. The hat stays Android's.
**Rejected**: taking all joystick motion and driving the hat too (the owner likes the hat as it
is; one more thing to get wrong).

### D11.2 · 2026-10-30 · Home's place across a restart (F11, audit F5)
Status: built 2026-10-30; not seen on the Portal (needs a low-memory kill mid-game).

**What**: each successful launch stores Home's place (category, focused app, row or dock) with
the boot's wall-clock instant; a launcher built again in the same boot (instant within 5 s)
reopens it, whatever *Home key goes to* says, and back in front the place is dropped.
**Rejected**: `Settings.Global.BOOT_COUNT` (a settings read for what the clock already says).
